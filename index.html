Who We Are

A credible technical partner for development, climate and agriculture programmes

Operating at the intersection of environmental science, rural development, and institutional capacity-building, GGR has earned recognition as a dependable technical partner for bilateral and multilateral development programmes, national government agencies, and private sector actors. Our technical scope covers the full programme cycle — from baseline vulnerability and environmental assessments, through design and implementation support, to monitoring, evaluation and policy advisory.
