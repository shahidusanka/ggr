Kano & Jigawa States2023 – Present

Green Legacy Limited — Technical Support Partnership

Client: Green Legacy Limited (agroforestry & environmental sustainability)

GGR's role: Lead technical support partner for vulnerability assessments, social impact evaluations, and ESG compliance.

Activities included systematic vulnerability assessments across Kano, Jigawa and adjacent states covering arid-land degradation and smallholder climate exposure; impact evaluations for the Kano Emirate Tree Planting Campaign and the Rail Trees Initiative; development of Green Legacy's ESG framework aligned to national safeguard standards and GCF-compatible reporting; and strategic advisory on the G.R.A.I.N. agro-cluster model along the LAKAJI economic corridor.

Consultancy
Kano, Katsina, Jigawa & Niger Republic2022

Kano–Maradi Railway Social Impact Assessment

Client: Mota-Engil (principal contractor)

GGR's role: Social Impact Assessment (SIA) across the full 400km rail corridor spanning Nigeria and Niger Republic.

Deployed socio-economic baseline surveys across multiple LGAs in Kano, Katsina and Jigawa States; identified vulnerable populations including smallholder farmers, women-headed households and nomadic herders; produced a comprehensive SIA report informing the Resettlement Action Plan (RAP), stakeholder engagement protocols and grievance redress mechanisms; and coordinated multi-level stakeholder engagement across 60+ communities, applying GIS-based spatial analysis along the 400km rail belt.
