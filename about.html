Who We Are

Company Overview

Gwarmai Global Resources Limited (GGR Ltd) is a specialized development consulting firm headquartered in Kano State, Nigeria (RC 1284957). Since its establishment, the company has delivered evidence-based, community-centred solutions for sustainable development, climate resilience, and inclusive growth across Nigeria's most climate-vulnerable regions.

GGR combines deep technical expertise in environmental management, social impact assessment, ESG compliance, and climate-smart agricultural practices with a proven ability to operate in complex, multi-stakeholder environments.

Operating at the intersection of environmental science, rural development, and institutional capacity-building, GGR has earned recognition as a credible and dependable technical partner for bilateral and multilateral development programmes, national government agencies, and private sector actors.

Background

Track record built on partnership

The firm's track record includes over three years of strategic partnership with Green Legacy Limited, a leading Northern Nigerian agroforestry and environmental sustainability company, in which GGR has served as the lead technical support partner for vulnerability assessments, social impact evaluations, and ESG compliance across Green Legacy's operations.

GGR's technical scope covers the full programme cycle: from baseline vulnerability and environmental assessments, through design, implementation support, monitoring and evaluation, to knowledge documentation and policy advisory.
