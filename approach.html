How We Work

Our Approach

GGR's technical scope covers the full programme cycle — from baseline assessment through to policy advisory.

01

Baseline & Vulnerability Assessment

We begin with evidence-based baseline and environmental assessments — including climate exposure and vulnerability assessments using participatory and geospatial methodologies — to establish context, risk and community priorities before any programme design begins.

02

Design

Findings from the assessment stage inform programme and safeguard design, including ESG framework development and the integration of gender-responsive and socially inclusive indicators.

03

Implementation Support

GGR works directly with relevant stakeholders and communities during delivery — from multi-level stakeholder engagement with government counterparts and traditional authorities, to farmer training and community mobilisation.

04

Monitoring & Evaluation

Community-based M&E systems, GIS and geospatial analysis, and baseline/midline/endline surveys track progress and performance against programme objectives.

05

Knowledge Documentation & Policy Advisory

Learning is documented into knowledge products for adaptive programme management, donor reporting, and policy advisory that scales successful approaches.

In Practice

Multi-stakeholder coordination

On the Kano–Maradi railway assignment, this approach meant coordinating engagement with state government counterparts, LGA authorities, community leaders including traditional title holders, civil society actors, and project-affected persons across more than 60 communities.
