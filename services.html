What We Do

Services

GGR's core competencies span the full technical scope required to design and deliver resilient, inclusive development programmes.

01

Disaster Risk Reduction & Climate Adaptation

  • Climate exposure and vulnerability assessments using participatory and geospatial methodologies
  • Risk profiling for agricultural communities in drought-prone, flood-risk, and land-degraded zones
  • Climate-smart agriculture design: agroforestry, Miyawaki food forest systems, half-moon water harvesting, dryland cropping
  • Environmental and Social Safeguards (ESS) compliance — World Bank ESF, AfDB, GCF, and EU standards
  • Resettlement Action Plans (RAPs) and livelihood restoration in infrastructure and climate-displacement contexts
02

ESG & Social Impact Assessment

  • Social Impact Assessments (SIA) covering land acquisition, displacement, community health, and livelihood disruption
  • ESG framework design, reporting, and third-party monitoring
  • Stakeholder engagement and grievance redress mechanism design
  • Environmental quality monitoring and watershed management assessments
03

Agricultural Value Chain & Livelihoods

  • Smallholder farmer productivity enhancement and climate-smart extension
  • Market linkage development and agricultural value chain strengthening
  • Agro-industrial cluster design and technical advisory (G.R.A.I.N. model)
  • Food security and nutrition-sensitive agriculture programming
  • Digital agricultural tools adoption and farmer capacity-building
04

Gender, Social Inclusion & Community Development

  • Gender-responsive and gender-transformative programme design
  • Women's economic empowerment and leadership development
  • Social cohesion and conflict-sensitive development in fragile contexts
  • Youth empowerment and skills training
  • Community-based participatory planning and monitoring
05

Monitoring, Evaluation & Knowledge Management

  • Community-based M&E systems and participatory evaluation
  • GIS, remote sensing, and geospatial analysis for environmental and social assessments
  • Baseline, midline, and endline surveys; impact evaluation frameworks
  • Learning documentation and knowledge products for adaptive programme management
